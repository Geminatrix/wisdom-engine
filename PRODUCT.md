# Wisdom Engine — Open Impact Engine Product

**Status**: Phase 1 scaffold · 2026-07-11  
**Parent**: LEPIE / Open Impact Engine (OIE)  
**Complements**: Starter Kit (meta blueprint)

## One-liner
A living system of testable Wisdom Units that turn insight into daily practice, sovereignty, and low-energy impact.

## What it is
A structured library of Wisdom Units — each one a five-field living insight:
`[WISDOM] · [WHY IT HOLDS] · [TEST] · [FAILURE MODE] · [UPGRADE PATH]`

Designed for people who want compressed, falsifiable guidance instead of motivational fluff. Built to be used by humans *and* by agentic systems (Pantheon-ready).
